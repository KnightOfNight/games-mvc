WHAT SHIPPED
------------

Three things, one coherent change, exactly as the brief scoped them.

1. THE LADDER IS EIGHT RUNGS, 96 DEFINITIONS.
   The seven unshipped rungs — silver, gold, platinum, rhodium, iridium,
   osmium, sphaerium — ship as 84 new accessory ItemDefinitions, twelve
   each (six stats x ring/amulet), each mirroring the v18 copper set
   exactly. Rungs 2-8 carry zero drop-table and zero vendor entries:
   Z02-Z08 do not exist yet, and seeding the ladder complete now is what
   the V24 new-zone-prep major is for.

2. TWO NEW ItemDefinition FIELDS (migration 0048).
   tier_material_mk_min / tier_material_mk_max, both nullable
   PositiveSmallIntegerField. A non-null minimum means the definition is a
   rung on the ladder; existing rows take NULL, correct by construction
   since only the seed authors membership. The migration is two AddFields
   plus the AlterField for the corrected suppress_mk_suffix help text —
   exactly as the brief predicted, generated by `make makemigrations
   APP=shyland`, unedited by hand, committed.

   Sphaerium is the terminal, unbounded rung: mk_max is NULL and it is the
   one rung that does NOT suppress its Mk suffix, because a rung with no
   ceiling cannot say its tier by material name alone. A sphaerium piece
   reads "Sphaerium Ring of Strength Mk 15".

   suppress_mk_suffix is un-overloaded: its help text now states it is a
   display flag and NOT ladder membership, independent in both directions.
   The freebie kit suppresses off the ladder; sphaerium joins the ladder
   without suppressing.

3. A HARD Mk-MISMATCH GUARD IN generate_item_instance.
   First statement in the function body, before any rolling. A ladder
   definition can no longer be instantiated outside its rung; the
   ValueError names the slug, the bound ("Mk 3" bounded, "Mk 8+"
   unbounded) and the offending tier. Both live generation paths funnel
   through this function — the loot-drop roll and do_buy's vendor path —
   so one guard binds them both plus every shell and admin caller.

Supporting work: admin's Identification fieldset gains both fields; the
copper twelve gain their range; the base_value back-fill loop extends over
all eight rungs at an unchanged 30; the seed's verification pass gains the
ladder's five invariants; the architecture doc is updated in place.

THE ONE-CURVE INVARIANT (the ruling's core) holds by construction: the 84
new definitions are generated from LADDER_MATERIALS x LADDER_STATS rather
than hand-copied, so every rung provably carries copper's stat authorship.
The engine's midpoint = base + factor x mk_tier does all the tier
progression — 2.8 at copper's Mk 1, 4.9 at silver's Mk 2, 15.4 at osmium's
Mk 7, 32.2 at sphaerium Mk 15, and upward without limit.

KNOWN, DELIBERATE RESIDUAL GAP (stated, not closed — brief §4)
---------------------------------------------------------------

Constructing an ItemInstance directly through the ORM bypasses the guard.
This stays open by ruling. A save()-time or clean()-time check would make
any pre-existing mismatched instance unsavable, turning a naming defect
into an unrepairable row — the scope law's leave-no-landmines clause.
Generation is the choke point that matters; the V24.14 incident came in
through generation.
