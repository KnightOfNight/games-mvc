DEVIATIONS FROM THE BRIEF
1. --max-tokens default is 5000, not the brief table's 1000 —
   operator-ruled in the playtest conversation (2026-08-24) after Sonnet
   5's default-on adaptive thinking (thinking counts against max_tokens)
   starved a think-heavy request into truncation, surfacing as silence.
2. Conversation persistence stores exchanges as plain text (request +
   final answer), not provider-shaped content blocks — deliberately
   provider-neutral (the brief's "structure reusable by future bots");
   within-request tool traffic is passed verbatim and not persisted.
3. The stub e2e (§8.3) was driven by a scripted play client under a
   dev-only test admin (sudo-e2e/Stubtester) rather than a browser — the
   brief offers "browser or the play client"; the scripted path is the
   objectively verifiable one.
4. Brief §5.1's log default named agents/sudo_bot.log relative to the repo;
   implemented relative to the agents/ directory (identical when run from
   the repo root; robust from anywhere).
5. Beyond-brief poli­sh, all operator-directed in the playtest: UTC-Z log
   stamps, logged pre-flight refusals, urllib3 warning filter, speaker
   stamp, location grammar, fresh-query and origin-capture prompt rules.
