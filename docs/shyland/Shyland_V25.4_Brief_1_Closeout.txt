   batch iteration" without specifying what _replay returns after severing.
   Implemented: the sever returns None and _stream treats None as
   "already closed", so the live tail never starts and no double-close is
   sent. Judged within the brief's intent (implementation detail, not a
   design rule); noted for completeness.
2. Test §9.3 (live sever) as literally specified ("observes close 4503
   after the switch engages") over-specified delivery order: the engage
   races the live loop's first top-of-iteration check, so the fed entry may
   or may not deliver before the close — both orders are correct behavior.
   The test asserts the guarantee (close 4503 arrives) tolerating either
   order. One suite run failed on the strict form before the fix; green
   after.
