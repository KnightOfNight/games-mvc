What shipped
------------
The composite strike: every equipped, non-broken weapon contributes to one strike
per round. New in combat_utils.py: PRIMARY_WEAPON_SLOT_PRIORITY = ('MAIN_HAND',
'RANGED', 'OFF_HAND'), SECONDARY_WEAPON_SLOT_FACTOR = {'OFF_HAND': 0.5, 'RANGED':
0.5}, SECONDARY_WEAPON_FACTOR_DEFAULT = 0.5 (named tunable constants — Phase 3
retunes by constants edit), and composite_weapon_term(weapons, eff_str, eff_dex):
per weapon, factor x (own midpoint±spread roll + own governing effective stat
[DEX if is_ranged else STR]) x own durability (1.0 - get_durability_penalty);
primary = first occupied slot in priority order at factor 1.0; unknown slots take
the 0.5 default, never a crash; no priority slot occupied = no primary, all
secondary. Tick engine player-attack path: equipped_weapons[0] retired — the
existing weapon filter feeds the helper, the term goes through the unchanged
calculate_damage as base_damage with stat_bonus=0 and durability_mod=1.0 (acuity
and graze/crit multiplier apply once, to the composite); armed/unarmed branches
on any-equipped-weapon at both the damage and flavor sites; unarmed else branch
byte-identical. One hit roll, one output line. Procs, lifesteal, gear crit,
equip resolver, and NPC-side combat untouched. No model changes, no migrations,
no seed-data changes, no client changes.
