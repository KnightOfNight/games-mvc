V24.13 BRIEF 1 CLOSEOUT REPORT — NPC HP Mk Scaling (#104)
=========================================================

SUMMARY
-------
NPC HP now scales with Mk tier at spawn time, per the #104 ruling of record
(2026-08-07) and the brief's six binding design rules. The helper
combat_utils.npc_max_vitality(npc_definition, mk_tier) computes
base_vitality × (1 + NPC_HP_BAND_LIFT × (mk_tier − 1)) with
NPC_HP_BAND_LIFT = 0.75, rounded half-up via int(lifted + 0.5) — never
built-in round(), whose banker's rounding sends .5 cases to even
(round(262.5) == 262, the #105 parity trap; Silk Matron 150 at Mk 2 is 263
by ruling and test-pinned as the sentinel). The tick engine's respawn sweep
(run_tick_engine.py, the codebase's only non-test NpcInstance creation
site — grep-verified still exactly one) computes hp once per spawn row and
persists it to both vitality_current and vitality_max; no read-time scaling
anywhere. Mk 1 multiplies by exactly 1 — zero change to any shipped spawn,
test-pinned as an identity invariant across base_vitality 1..1000. The lift
is linear per band and uniform across all combat tiers (normals, elites,
bosses — deliberately not exponential); base_vitality remains the authored
within-band value carrying species identity and the #101 boss-ladder tuning.
Doctrine: at-level time-to-kill is band-invariant. Opens Phase 3 (Mk 2
balance) of the V24 series plan (#139).
