V24.10 BRIEF 1 CLOSEOUT REPORT — The Proc Floor (#127)
=======================================================

SUMMARY
-------
Floored proc composition implemented per the brief's binding design rules (verified
against rulings (a)-(j) on #127 and GDD 239159a). The floor is authored, deterministic,
rarity-blind: X = floor_base + (floor_factor × mk_tier), snapshotted as an int 'floor'
key on the instance's rolled primary entry at drop time. Proc success on a floored
entry pays randint(X, X + ceil(V)); unfloored entries keep the shipped
randint(1, ceil(V)) expression byte-identical (two paths, selected by key presence).
Proc chance is untouched — min(0.50, V × 0.05), reads V only, both paths.
flame_factor joined PROC_FACTOR_STATS as its fourth member (flavor name only).
Examine renders the promise parenthetical "(between X and Y damage)" on floored
entries only, via the new shared list-blind helper SkylandConsumer._item_stat_line.
