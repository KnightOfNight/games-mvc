Authoring note (recorded, not a deviation): the three integer-bar terminal
lines carry the actual-delta parenthetical. Rationale: for those branches a
partial application (actual < nominal) occurs exactly and only on the arrival
tick — the clamp only bites at the boundary — so the brief's §6 requirements
"arrival announces the terminal line" and "partial application announces the
partial delta, not the nominal" are the same tick; the delta parenthetical on
the terminal line satisfies both. The two acuity terminals are bare, exactly
like the shift branches', since their ordinary lines announce the resulting
value (already actual) rather than a delta.

Operator playtest recipes (brief §9 — dev stack, ready now)

Effects are applied via the Django shell (`make shell` from the worktree).
Paste this prelude first, setting your character's name:

    from datetime import timedelta
    from django.utils import timezone
    from apps.shyland.models import (Character, EffectDefinition,
        EffectComponent, EffectInstance, EffectComponentInstance)

    CHAR = Character.objects.get(name__iexact='YOUR-CHARACTER-NAME')

    def apply_tick_effect(ctype, magnitude, minutes=10):
        d, _ = EffectDefinition.objects.get_or_create(
            slug=f"playtest-{ctype.replace('_', '-')}",
            defaults={'name': f'Playtest {ctype}'})
        c, _ = EffectComponent.objects.get_or_create(
            definition=d, component_type=ctype, order=0,
            defaults={'magnitude_base': magnitude, 'magnitude_scaling': 0.0,
                      'duration_base': 600.0, 'duration_scaling': 0.0})
        ei = EffectInstance.objects.create(
            definition=d, target=CHAR, mk_tier=1, is_active=True)
        EffectComponentInstance.objects.create(
            effect_instance=ei, component=c, magnitude=magnitude,
            is_active=True,
            expires_at=timezone.now() + timedelta(minutes=minutes))
        return ei

Format rule (operator, 2026-09-13): every step is an explicit numbered

Checklist step numbers match brief §9.

STEP 1 — silent full-bar HoT (this is also verification §7.3):
  1. Move to a room with no aggressive NPCs (The Convergence works) and
     stay out of combat.
  2. Confirm Vitality is full (wait for regen, or run:
         Character.objects.filter(pk=CHAR.pk).update(
             vitality_current=CHAR.vitality_max) ).
  3. Run:  apply_tick_effect('hot_vitality', 15)
  4. Watch the game pane for at least 15 seconds (>=4 round boundaries).
     Expected: no messages of any kind from the ticker.
  5. If you see ANY "You recover ... Vitality" line while the bar is full,
     stop and report back. If you see nothing, step passes — run the
     teardown and continue.
  (In a room with aggro/respawns you WILL take damage and the HoT will
  correctly announce real heals — that is not this step; use a safe room.)

STEP 2 — HoT recovery + one terminal line:
  1. Out of combat, run:
         Character.objects.filter(pk=CHAR.pk).update(vitality_current=40)
  2. Run:  apply_tick_effect('hot_vitality', 500)
     (magnitude 500 so the HoT always performs the arrival itself — with a
     small magnitude, silent passive regen can complete the bar between
     boundaries instead, in which case the HoT correctly holds silent and
     no terminal line ever fires; doctrine-correct but not this step's
     demonstration.)
  3. Watch the pane for ~5 seconds.
     Expected: exactly ONE "Your body is whole once more. (+N Vitality)"
     (N = the actual gap filled), then nothing.
  4. If the line repeats, or a "You recover ..." line appears at the full
     bar, stop and report back. Otherwise the step passes — run the
     teardown and continue.

STEP 3 — dot_longevity floor:
  1. Enter combat with any NPC and stay in it for this step (in combat,
     passive longevity regen pauses; out of combat it restores +1 point
     every ~8-36s, and the still-active DoT legitimately re-drains it with
     a "... (-1 Longevity)" bled-dry line each time — doctrine-correct but
     it muddies the silence check).
  2. Run:  apply_tick_effect('dot_longevity', 40)
  3. Watch the pane. Expected, in order:
       a. "Your stamina drains from Playtest dot_longevity. (-40 Longevity)"
          each round while above the floor (actual delta, category combat);
       b. exactly ONE "Your stamina is bled dry. (-N Longevity)" on the
          arrival tick, where N is the actual remaining amount (partial,
          not the nominal 40, unless the bar was an exact multiple);
       c. then silence from this effect while it remains active.
  4. If the bled-dry line repeats while you stay in combat, or any drain
     line shows the nominal magnitude when less was actually applied, stop
     and report back. Otherwise the step passes — run the teardown, leave
     combat, and continue.

STEP 4 — hot_acuity at baseline (re-verified after the #332 fix):
  1. Out of combat, wait until your Acuity has settled, then confirm it
     equals your baseline exactly:
         print(CHAR.acuity_current, CHAR.acuity_baseline)
  2. Run:  apply_tick_effect('hot_acuity', 0.1)
  3. Watch the pane for at least 15 seconds.
     Expected: no messages at all (at baseline the effect is a no-op).
  4. If you see any "Your mind clears ..." line, stop and report back.
     If you see nothing, continue:
  5. Run:  Character.objects.filter(pk=CHAR.pk).update(acuity_current=0.70)
  6. Watch the pane. Expected, in order:
       a. per-round "Your mind clears from Playtest hot_acuity.
          (Acuity 0.80)" then "(Acuity 0.90)" (numerals may sit a
          hundredth or two off — passive drift also nudges the value);
       b. exactly ONE "Your mind settles back to its center.";
       c. then silence, with the acuity bar resting exactly at your
          baseline (not flapping).
  7. If the settles line repeats, or clears lines continue after it, or
     the bar oscillates, stop and report back. Otherwise the step passes —
     run the teardown and continue.

STEP 5 — dot_vitality unchanged:
  1. Run:  apply_tick_effect('dot_vitality', 2)
  2. Watch the pane. Expected each round:
     "You take 2 damage from Playtest dot_vitality." — exactly the
     pre-26.0 wording, category combat.
  3. If the wording differs in any way from what damage ticks have always
     said, stop and report back. Otherwise the step passes — run the
     teardown (before the bar gets low).

STEP 6 — small-magnitude hot_acuity walks at two decimals (added by
operator direction 2026-09-13 after the #332 part-2 fix):
  1. Out of combat, run:  apply_tick_effect('hot_acuity', 0.01)
  2. Run:  Character.objects.filter(pk=CHAR.pk).update(acuity_current=0.70)
  3. Watch the pane and the acuity bar for ~60 seconds.
     Expected: the bar climbs steadily (never flaps backwards); per-round
     "Your mind clears from Playtest hot_acuity. (Acuity 0.7x/0.8x/...)"
     lines with two-decimal values making real progress each round
     (drift adds its own silent progress between rounds, so the pace is
     faster than 0.01 per round); then exactly ONE "Your mind settles
     back to its center."; then silence with the bar resting exactly at
     your baseline.
  4. If the bar ever moves away from baseline, or the value flaps, or the
     settles line repeats, stop and report back. Otherwise the step
     passes — run the teardown.
